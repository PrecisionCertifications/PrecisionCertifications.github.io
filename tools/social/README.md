## Wording rules (required)
- The company is an independent training company. Never call it an "IPC training company/center" or imply it is an IPC Licensed Training Center.
- The instructor is "a Certified IPC Trainer". Do not mention CIT or CSE certifications otherwise.
- Training is CIS certification and recertification only.
- Never claim training on clients' own products or processes, except "custom courses built around your processes, on request".
- No specific discount percentages. No made-up stats, testimonials or claims of demand.
- Keep IPC facts general and accurate; avoid specific numeric acceptance criteria.
