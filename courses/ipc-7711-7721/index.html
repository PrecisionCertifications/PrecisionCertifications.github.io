IPC-7711/7721 · CIS certification

Rework, Modification and Repair of Electronic Assemblies

IPC-7711/7721 lays out proven methods for removing and replacing components and repairing boards and circuitry with as little impact on quality and reliability as possible.

This is an advanced class. Roughly 85% of it is hands-on bench work that is performed and graded in class.

Request a quote

Who it’s for

Rework and repair technicians who already solder well, including fine-pitch surface mount work.

Recertification

Currently certified? Recertify within 6 months before your expiration date to stay current without a gap. The class reviews the procedures and policy updates, and students demonstrate their hands-on skills again.

At a glance
Class length
4 to 4.5 days, depending on skill level
Prerequisite
Solid soldering skills, including fine pitch
Exams
Open-book written exam (70% to pass), plus a hands-on practical for each optional module
Students receive
The IPC-7711/7721 standard, rework and repair kits, and an IPC CIS certificate
Certification term
2 years
