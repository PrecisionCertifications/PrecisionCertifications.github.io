IPC/WHMA-A-620 · CIS certification

Requirements and Acceptance for Cable and Harness Assemblies

IPC/WHMA-A-620 is the industry's standard for cable and wire harness assemblies. Students learn to apply it to accept and reject decisions, and to use the same terms as the customers and auditors who rely on it.

The course is modular, so a crimping technician and a coax specialist can each take what applies to their work.

Request a quote

Who it’s for

Harness builders, cable technicians, inspectors and quality staff working with wire harnesses and cable assemblies.

Course structure

Every student takes the required introduction, then picks the optional modules that fit their work.

Introduction, wire preparation, measuring and testing cable assembliesRequired4.5 hours
Crimp terminations and insulation displacement connectionsOptional3.25 hours
Soldered terminationsOptional3.5 hours
Connectors, molding and pottingOptional2.5 hours
Ultrasonic welding and splicesOptional2 hours
Marking and labeling, wire bundle securing and final installationOptional3.5 hours
Coaxial and biaxial cable assembliesOptional2 hours
Electrical shielding and protective coveringsOptional2 hours

Hands-on add-on

A 1 to 1.5 day bench session where students prepare wire, build and inspect crimps, splices and solder terminations, and choose the right wire size and tooling. Practical exams require 80% to pass. It is taken with or within 90 days after the base A-620 certification, and students should already have basic cable-building experience. Completion is noted on the base certificate.

Space Addendum

For cable and harness assemblies built for space and military hardware. See Space Addendum details.
