# Precision Certifications and Consulting

**Website: [precisioncertifications.com](https://precisioncertifications.com)**

Precision Certifications and Consulting is an independent training company based in North Alabama. We provide on-site IPC CIS certification and recertification for technicians, inspectors and engineers, taught by a Certified IPC Trainer with hands-on industry experience.

## Courses
- IPC J-STD-001: Requirements for Soldered Electrical and Electronic Assemblies
- IPC-A-610: Acceptability of Electronic Assemblies
- IPC/WHMA-A-620: Requirements and Acceptance for Cable and Harness Assemblies
- IPC-7711/7721: Rework, Modification and Repair of Electronic Assemblies
- IPC-A-600: Acceptability of Printed Boards
- IPC-6012: Qualification and Performance of Rigid Printed Boards
- Space Addenda for J-STD-001 and IPC/WHMA-A-620
